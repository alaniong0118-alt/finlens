Method and limits

TF-IDF word unigrams/bigrams + LinearSVC, balanced classes, C=10. Fits only the original 2,071 training rows from Financial PhraseBank 75Agree. Repeat submissions reuse the fitted model.

Dataset and label order
Financial PhraseBank v1.0, 75Agree (more than 75% annotation agreement), ISO-8859-1; 3,453 source rows and 3,349 text groups. Label order: negative, neutral, positive. Original labels retained; consensus subsets were not combined.

Parser and grouping
phrasebank-pinned-latin1-v1; nfkc-numeric-jaccard-sequence-v1. Duplicate/near-duplicate grouping changes grouping keys only, not model input or labels.
Split provenance
StratifiedGroupKFold, five folds, shuffle enabled, seed 42. Training folds 2–4: 2,071 rows; validation fold 1: 691 rows; test fold 0: 691 rows. Defined group-overlap checks passed in the historical reproduction.
Split fingerprint SHA256

Training and selection
Training-only TF-IDF word unigrams/bigrams and balanced LinearSVC, seed 42. C=10 selected by validation macro-F1 from 0.1, 1 and 10; ties prefer smaller C. No validation refit; test predictions occurred after selection. The baseline always predicts the training-majority label, neutral.
Historical environment
Windows x64; Python 3.14.7; Unicode 16.0.0; scikit-learn 1.9.1; NumPy 2.5.3; SciPy 1.18.1; joblib 1.6.0; threadpoolctl 3.7.0. Reproduction in other environments is not established.

One small, imbalanced, agreement-filtered financial-news corpus and one held-out partition limit generalization. Original sentence labels are historical annotations, not objective current issuer sentiment. Retained duplicate weighting and heuristic grouping do not establish event, issuer or time independence, or eliminate every possible overlap. These results do not establish SEC-filing suitability, calibrated probabilities, return predictability or production readiness. No corpus sentences, row-linked predictions, vocabulary or weights are provided; private noncommercial academic-demo limits and the attribution below continue to apply.

  • Financial-news sentence sentiment only. No SEC Filing Tone replacement or SEC-filing validation.
  • No cybersecurity-risk classification, calibrated probabilities, trading signals or stock/portfolio return predictions.
  • No production-readiness or cross-company generalization claim. The model can misclassify unfamiliar or ambiguous wording.
  • Private, nonprofit academic use only. No online deployment, corpus distribution or trained-weight redistribution is authorized.
